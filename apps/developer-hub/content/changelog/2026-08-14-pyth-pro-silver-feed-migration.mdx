The active silver index is now `Metal.Index.SILVER/USD` in the public Pyth Pro
catalog. The older `Crypto.Index.SILV/USD` symbol is inactive and should not be
used for new integrations.

**Why it matters:** moving silver consumers to the metal taxonomy keeps
subscriptions, dashboards, and catalog lookups aligned with the active feed.

**What to do:** update new silver subscriptions and feed lookups to
`Metal.Index.SILVER/USD`. Do not start new work on `Crypto.Index.SILV/USD`;
existing Metal.Index.SILVER/USD consumers are already on the active feed.
