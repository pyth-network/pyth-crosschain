---
title: Flow EVM Mainnet joins the Pyth Pro-compatible contract upgrade
date: 2026-08-04
product: pyth-pro
type: feature
area: contracts
---

Flow EVM Mainnet is now covered by the Pyth Pro-compatible contract upgrade.
Apps on Flow EVM Mainnet can point their integrations at the upgraded Pyth
contract and consume the same Pyth Pro-backed price updates already available
on other upgraded EVM chains.

Why it matters: the upgraded contract is the single integration surface for
Pyth on EVM going forward, and adding Flow EVM Mainnet keeps Flow apps aligned
with the same feed catalog and update semantics as the rest of the upgraded
network. Legacy Flow EVM Mainnet consumers keep working — the previous Pyth
contract address is unchanged.

What to do: if you are starting a new integration on Flow EVM Mainnet, use the
upgraded (Pro-compatible) address. If you are migrating an existing Flow app,
follow the standard EVM migration steps.

- [EVM contract addresses (upgraded and current)](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/contract-addresses/evm)
- [Migrate an app to Pyth](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/migrate-an-app-to-pyth)
