date: 2026-08-13
product: pyth-core
type: docs
---

Oracle Integrity Staking pages now show persistent notices that OIS rewards are
paused. The notices also state that staking and slashing remain active, stake is
still at risk of slashing, and governance staking is not affected.

**Why it matters:** OIS participants can distinguish the paused rewards program
from the still-active slashing risk before leaving stake assigned.

**What to do:** review your assigned OIS stake and risk exposure. You can
unstake or withdraw if you do not want OIS exposure while rewards are paused;
governance staking is unaffected.
