title: XAGM/USD.RR sponsored push feed available on Sui mainnet
date: 2026-08-07
product: pyth-pro
type: feature
area: market-data
---

Sui mainnet apps can now consume a sponsored `XAGM/USD.RR` push feed without
running their own price pusher. The feed is Pro-compatible and updates on the
sponsored Sui push schedule alongside the rest of the Sui sponsored list.

Why it matters: sponsored push feeds are automatically kept fresh on-chain by
Pyth infrastructure, so integrators do not have to build or operate a pusher
just to read a live price on Sui.

What to do: read `XAGM/USD.RR` on Sui the same way as any other sponsored feed
— point at the standard Sui Pyth price feed object and use the feed ID listed
on the push-feeds page. Existing feed integrations are unchanged.
