The Entropy chainlist no longer shows eight networks scheduled to leave
Entropy support on **August 15, 2026**: **ZetaChain, Unichain, Taiko, Tabi,
Story, Blast, Etherlink, and Sei EVM** (mainnet and testnet).

Why it matters: apps depending on Entropy randomness on these networks will
stop receiving reveals after the support end date. The chainlist change makes
sure new integrators do not pick up a network that will not be maintained
past August 15.

What to do: if your application uses Entropy on any of the eight chains
above, plan a migration before **August 15, 2026**. See the deprecation
notice for the full timeline and the rationale.
