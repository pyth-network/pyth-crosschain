---
title: SUIUSDE/USD sponsored push feed available on Sui mainnet
date: 2026-08-12
product: pyth-pro
type: feature
area: market-data
---

Sui mainnet apps can now consume a sponsored `SUIUSDE/USD` push feed without
running their own price pusher. The feed is listed as available on the Sui
sponsored feed page with a 10-second heartbeat and 0.5% price-deviation
threshold.

**Why it matters:** sponsored push feeds are automatically kept fresh on-chain
by Pyth infrastructure, so integrators can read a live SUIUSDE price on Sui
without operating a pusher.

**What to do:** read `SUIUSDE/USD` on Sui the same way as any other sponsored
feed - point at the standard Sui Pyth price feed object and use the feed ID
listed on the push-feeds page. Existing feed integrations are unchanged.
